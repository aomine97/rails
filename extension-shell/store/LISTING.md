## Store listing tab
Name (from manifest): Rails: autofill job applications
Summary (132 chars max):

Description:
Rails fills job applications from a profile you confirmed once: your resume, your standard answers, your work authorization. It works on Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, iCIMS, SmartRecruiters and most company career pages.

What it does
- Fills every field it can from your Rails profile, then shows you what it filled and what it skipped.
- Never clicks Submit. You review, you submit.
- Standard answers and EEO responses stay in your browser. Rails never stores them on a server.
- Logs the application in your Rails tracker so you never lose track of where you applied.

Early access: this version opens the panel and links to your Rails profile. Autofill ships in the next update.
